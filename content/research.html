Geotechnical earthquake engineering, multi-hazard infrastructure risk, and coastal processes.

2025–PRESENT · RUTGERS UNIVERSITY

Unified seismic slope displacement modeling for subduction earthquakes

Developing a unified probabilistic and data-driven framework for earthquake-induced slope displacement under interface and intraslab subduction earthquakes.

The work integrates nonlinear site response, sliding-block analysis, seismic hazard concepts, and machine-learning models using the NGA-Subduction database.

Advisor: Tyler J. Oathes, Ph.D. Collaboration with Trevor J. Carey, Ph.D., University of Toronto.

Ongoing research · related manuscript in preparation
2026–PRESENT · RUTGERS UNIVERSITY

Probabilistic seismic–flood risk assessment of levee systems

Developing a probabilistic framework to quantify increased flood risk following earthquake-induced levee damage and loss of freeboard.

The framework incorporates seismic and flood hazards, fragility modeling, hazard disaggregation, sensitivity analysis, and uncertainty quantification for multi-hazard infrastructure assessment.

Advisor: Tyler J. Oathes, Ph.D. Collaboration with Trevor J. Carey, Ph.D., University of Toronto, and Efthymios I. Nikolopoulos, Ph.D., Rutgers University.

Ongoing research · related manuscript in preparation
2026–PRESENT · CAIT, RUTGERS UNIVERSITY

Newark Bay coastal resiliency modeling

Developing Delft3D hydrodynamic and sediment-transport models to assess sediment dynamics, marsh degradation, and navigational channel stability in Newark Bay.

Research conducted through the Center for Advanced Infrastructure and Transportation (CAIT), in collaboration with Daniel Barone, Ph.D., and Robert Miskewitz, Ph.D., Rutgers University.

2023 · UNIVERSITY OF CUENCA

Debris-flow susceptibility assessment

Undergraduate thesis investigating two micro-watersheds in the Marianza sector following the March 2022 Sayausí–Cuenca debris-flow event. The work combined field investigations, geological and geomorphological characterization, soil classification, hydrometeorological analysis, HEC-HMS rainfall–runoff modeling, and susceptibility mapping using the RES methodology.

Advisor: Vladimiro Tobar, Ph.D. Technical support: Aleksandr Khachatryan, Geol., and Marco Ramírez, M.Sc. Thesis conducted jointly with J. Chaca.
