Beyond research

Originally from Paute, Ecuador, I value education, perseverance, and the opportunities created by working with others. I believe in going the extra mile: asking one more question, taking the time to understand a difficult problem, and supporting those around me. These values guide my growth as a person and an engineer, and motivate me to use what I learn to help communities address natural hazards.
